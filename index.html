Kya Banaye — Shuddh Shakahari

Kya Banaye is a smart, offline-first Progressive Web App designed specifically for Indian vegetarian households. It ends the daily question "आज क्या बनाएं?" (What should we cook today?) with zero stress and thoughtful variety.

🛡️
3-Day Anti-Repeat Cooldown

Whenever you tap "आज बनाया?", that dish is locked out of daily shuffles for 3 days to guarantee variety.

🌙
Kal Ki Taiyari (Advance Prep)

Nightly reminder at 8:30 PM to soak pulses, set dahi, or prep ingredients, saving 30 minutes in the morning rush.

📅
Saptahik Bhojan Yojana

A balanced 7-day vegetarian rotation covering high-protein dals, sabzis, and seasonal home favorites.

📖
Rasoi Diary (Kitchen Diary)

Automatically records what you cooked and when, providing an organized record of household meals.
